BandLock

English

BandLock is a jailbreak tweak that lets you inspect and manually control the LTE/4G bands allowed by the iPhone modem directly from Settings. It supports manual band selection, Spain-focused presets, Automatic and LTE/4G-only network modes, restoration of all modem-supported LTE bands, CoreTelephony post-write verification with a safe retry, and direct access to FTMInternal / Field Test Mode.

BandLock has no resident daemon, does not inject into SpringBoard and does not automatically change modem settings at boot. The modem and carrier still decide the final serving band and carrier aggregation among the allowed bands.

Highlights

  • Manual LTE band selection (including B1, B3, B7, B8, B20, B28 and B38 where supported)
  • Automatic / LTE-4G-only mode
  • Restore previous selection or all supported LTE bands
  • CoreTelephony verification and one automatic retry when CommCenter has not committed yet
  • FTMInternal / Field Test launcher and diagnostic logs

Español

BandLock es un tweak de jailbreak que permite consultar y controlar manualmente desde Ajustes las bandas LTE/4G permitidas por el módem del iPhone. Incluye selección manual de bandas, perfiles orientados a España, modos Automático y Solo LTE/4G, restauración de todas las bandas LTE soportadas por el módem, verificación posterior mediante CoreTelephony con un reintento seguro y acceso directo a FTMInternal / Field Test Mode.

No utiliza un daemon residente, no se inyecta en SpringBoard y no cambia automáticamente la configuración del módem al arrancar. El módem y el operador siguen decidiendo la banda servidora final y la agregación de portadoras entre las bandas permitidas.
