Erika Reborn 2.0.2

Erika Reborn is an updated fork of Paisseon's Erika package downloader for Sileo. Maintained by GokuEn with RootHide compatibility, updated Sileo integration, and a version selector combining public APT packages with Erika's online archive.

Credits

Paisseon: original creator of Erika.
GokuEn: fork updates, RootHide support and archive version selection.

Packages

Version 2.0.2 for RootHide (iphoneos-arm64e) and rootless (iphoneos-arm64). All interface messages are in English. Compatibility varies by device, jailbreak, and Sileo version; it is not restricted to a single iOS release.

Important limitation

Erika Reborn can now download free public APT versions even when they are not present in the Chomikuj archive, verifying SHA-256 and Debian file structure. Archive-only versions still require the provider's authorization, which may include login and reCAPTCHA; open the original page or provide an authorized HTTPS link. No licensing or access controls are bypassed.
